## Project Overview

This project analyzes the Olist Brazilian e-commerce marketplace dataset
covering 2016–2018, spanning multiple joined data tables including orders,
customers, sellers, products, payments, and geolocation. The goal was to
uncover actionable business insights around sales performance, customer
behavior, seller dynamics, product category revenue, and regional distribution
across Brazil's marketplace ecosystem.

**Deliverables:** Python analysis notebooks + rewritten SQL queries +
full report + presentation

---

## Key Findings

**Revenue & Sellers**
- Revenue follows a classic Pareto distribution — roughly 20% of sellers
  generate 80% of total marketplace revenue
- Top-performing sellers maintain better ratings, faster delivery times,
  and broader product catalogs

**Top Revenue Categories**
- Health & Beauty is the highest revenue category, driven by repeat
  purchases and strong customer retention
- Watches & Gifts commands premium price points with seasonal spikes
  during holidays
- Bed, Bath & Table delivers stable year-round volume due to household
  replenishment demand

**Highest AOV Categories**
- Computers hold the highest Average Order Value across all categories
  (laptops, desktops, accessories)
- Appliances are the second-highest AOV segment, representing a key
  growth opportunity

**Customer Behaviour**
- São Paulo leads with 42% of total customers; Rio de Janeiro (13%)
  and Minas Gerais (12%) follow
- Average customer places 1.03 orders with an AOV of R$154
- Peak ordering occurs on weekdays, especially Monday–Wednesday afternoons
- Only 3% of customers made repeat purchases — a significant retention
  opportunity

**Geographic Distribution**
- Southeast Brazil dominates marketplace activity
- Coastal states generate 78% of total marketplace revenue
- Urban centers receive faster shipping; remote regions face longer
  transit times
